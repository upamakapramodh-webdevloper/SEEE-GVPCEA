About SEEE

The official student club of Electrical & Electronics Engineering, GVPCE(A).

Who We Are

To create a dynamic environment where students gain hands-on experience, explore advanced technologies, and grow as professionals in Electrical & Electronics Engineering.

To organize workshops, industrial visits, technical events, and training programs that bridge the gap between academics and real-world engineering.
